Research

  • 07.2026

    Master thesis, ETH Zurich

    Advisor: Prof. Dr. Lorenz Halbeisen

    Improving Bounds on Hales–Jewett Numbers. A symmetry reduction turned an intractable search over more than 10 billion points into a small constraint model (253 variables, 1,771 constraints) that CryptoMiniSat solves in seconds. Every bound is independently verified by code. Interactive companion: Hales–Jewett explorer.

  • 06.2026

    Preprint

    arXiv:2606.22155, math.CO, sole author

    Lower Bounds for the Hales–Jewett Numbers via Symmetric and One-Weight Colorings. New records: HJ(3,3) from 14 to 22 and HJ(4,2) from 12 to 14.
